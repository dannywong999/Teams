# Architelier correspondence template

Use this structure for letters and formal memos. Replace bracketed fields. Do not invent a seal, permit number, or legal description.

```text
[Date: Month D, YYYY]

Attention: [Name / municipality / firm]
[Company]
[Address]

Project Address: [civic address, including unit suffix if required]
Project: [short name]

RE: [specific subject]

[Opening: why this letter exists in one or two sentences.]

[Body: facts, what was reviewed, what is confirmed, what is requested.
Use short paragraphs. Cite drawings, code sections, or prior letters by title and date.]

[Closing: contact line.]

Please contact the undersigned if further clarification is required.

DANNY WONG
Architect AIBC, MRAIC, RI, B.Arch., LEED AP
(672) 727-8288
ARCHITELIER ARCHITECTURE & REAL ESTATE CONSULTING INC.
Office: 680 – 838 West Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC V6C 0A6
Mailing Address: 7430 Granville Street, Vancouver, BC V6P 0G1
www.architelier.com
```

## Notes

- Prefer this phone number unless a newer signature is already on the thread.
- City letters: no first-name sign-off; no emoji; no “happy to help.”
- Consultant email may be slightly shorter but still uses the same signature block.
- Save project-specific letters as Gmail drafts or Drive docs. Only commit a template change to this repo when the office format itself changed.
